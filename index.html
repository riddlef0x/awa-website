About the show

Two operators. Real businesses. No hype.

Robin Leonard and Tobi Webster run AI agents inside real businesses every day. Each episode digs into what's shipping in agentic AI right now: the tools, the launches, the failures, and the uncomfortable questions.

What should an agent be allowed to do on its own? How do you audit an AI employee? And when it goes wrong, whose fault is it? If you're a founder, operator, or builder working out how much rope to give your agents, this is the show.
